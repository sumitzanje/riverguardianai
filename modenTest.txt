PROJECT
============================================================

RiverGuardian AI

Purpose:
A solar-powered river water-level monitoring and flood-warning system.

Deployment location:
A river bridge near Karjat, Maharashtra, India.

Core functions:

- Read river water level using an ultrasonic sensor.
- Process measurements locally.
- Calculate clearance, rise rate, risk level and time to unsafe level.

- Store local readings.
- Upload readings and device health to Supabase when LTE is available.
- Display data on an existing dashboard.

- Automatically restart after power failure, software crash or modem reconnect.
